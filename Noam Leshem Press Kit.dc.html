01 / Biography

No barrier between her and the dancefloor.

Noam Leshem is a Tel Aviv-based DJ and producer whose sets move between psychedelic psytrance, deep techno, and electronic grooves. She started as a singer · music has been her first language since childhood · and that background shapes the way she builds a set: not track by track, but as one continuous arc.

She came up through the Tel Aviv electronic scene, producing alongside T-PUSE, Sharon Attar, and Guy Davidov. Over time the heavier, more psychedelic end of the spectrum started pulling her in, and she followed it. That path took her to some of the biggest stages in the local and international scene: Burning Man, Boom, Midburn, IMAGiNE, Hilltop, Darwish Land, River Riders. And beyond Israel to the US, India, Thailand, Greece, Portugal, and the UAE.

In the last year she has been sharing stages with leading psytrance artists including Dekel, Shaiman, and Racing Dust, with a standout performance at Hilltop in Goa.
